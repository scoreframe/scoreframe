1. These terms

These Club Terms are an agreement between Glavin Labs LLC, which makes ScoreFrame ("we", "us"), and the organization named on the club's account (the "Club"). They cover club licenses bought on scoreframe.app. ScoreFrame's general Terms of Service and Privacy Policy also apply to everyone who uses ScoreFrame; where they conflict with these Club Terms on a club matter, these Club Terms control.

2. Who agrees

The person who creates the club, or accepts these terms in the club setup, confirms that they are authorized to agree for the Club. That person is the club's director. The Club is responsible for the directors, coaches and managers it invites, and for keeping its director account secure.

3. Licenses

  • A license covers one team for the period bought. A Club can't have more active teams than it has licenses.
  • Everyone on a covered team's staff (director, coaches and managers) gets ScoreFrame Pro features while using that team. It doesn't change their plan for other teams.
  • The director can move licenses between the Club's teams. Licenses can't be resold or used for teams outside the Club.
  • The minimum purchase is 3 teams.

4. Free trial

A new Club can try ScoreFrame free for 14 days with up to 3 teams. Each organization gets one trial. If a trial looks like a repeat of an earlier one, we may ask for a card before it starts; the card isn't charged during the trial. When the trial ends without a purchase, see section 10.

5. Prices and payment

  • Prices are in US dollars, per team per month, and are shown before you buy. Buying 6 months or more costs less per month.
  • The price per team you first buy at is locked for 12 months from that first purchase, including when you add teams or renew in that time.
  • Sales tax is added where it applies. Payments are processed by Stripe; we don't store card or bank details.
  • Card payments activate licenses right away. US bank payments cover up to 3 teams while the payment clears (usually a few business days), then all licensed teams.

6. Invoices

A Club can ask to pay by invoice, for example with a purchase order. Invoices are due within 30 days. Until an invoice is paid, up to 3 of the Club's teams are covered; the rest are covered once it's paid. If an invoice is still unpaid 14 days after its due date, coverage from it stops until it's paid. We may agree in writing to cover all teams before payment, for example for a school district.

7. Ending and renewing

Licenses end on the date shown and don't renew by themselves. We email the director before coverage ends. The Club can renew, extend or add teams at any time on its club page.

8. Refunds

There are no automatic refunds. If something isn't working, contact us first: we'll work to fix it quickly. We consider refund requests individually and may refund part of a license, prorated by unused time and licenses. A full refund ends the licenses it paid for.

9. Your data

The Club owns the content it and its staff add to ScoreFrame: team names, rosters, schedules, scores, stats, notes and logos ("Club Content"). The Club gives us permission to store, process and display Club Content to provide ScoreFrame, following the Club's privacy settings. We don't sell Club Content. The director can ask us for an export of the Club's data at any time.

10. Keeping and deleting data

  • A trial that ends without a purchase: the Club's team pages become private when the trial ends. We keep the data for 30 days so the Club can buy and carry on, and the director can download it. After that it's deleted.
  • A paid Club whose licenses end: team pages stay up, read-only, for 12 months (clubs often pause between seasons). After that we email the director, keep the data 30 more days, and then delete it unless the Club renews.
  • Coaches' and families' own ScoreFrame accounts, and teams that aren't the Club's, are not affected. Videos on YouTube are controlled by their channel owners, not by us.

11. Privacy settings

The director chooses who can see the Club's team pages, game pages and tournament pages: only followers a coach approves, anyone signed in to ScoreFrame, or anyone with the link. New clubs start at "anyone signed in to ScoreFrame". Each team follows the club setting unless it's made more private; coaches can make their own team more private but not more open. Note that anyone can create a free ScoreFrame account, so "signed in" limits casual sharing and keeps pages out of search engines, but it is not the same as approving each person.

12. Children

Club teams often include children. Before adding a child's name, photo, video, jersey number or stats, the Club must have permission from the child's parent or guardian as required by applicable law, and must honor a parent's request to remove their child's information. ScoreFrame accounts are for people 13 and older; children's information should be entered by Club staff, not by children. The Club is responsible for choosing privacy settings suitable for its players.

13. YouTube

Games are streamed and stored on YouTube channels the Club or its staff control. Connecting a channel on the club page lets ScoreFrame create live streams on that channel for the Club's teams; the connection is kept on our servers and is not shown to coaches. The Club can disconnect at any time on the club page or in its Google account settings. YouTube's Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy apply to anything on YouTube. The Club is responsible for its channel's settings and for having the right to record and stream its games, including venue rules and music.

14. AI tools

Some features read schedules, rosters and tournament draws from text, photos or files using AI services provided by third parties. Files are sent to those services only to produce the import and aren't used by us for anything else. Results can be wrong: review imports before relying on them. To keep costs fair, AI features have usage limits.

15. Results from other services

ScoreFrame can import schedules and results from public tournament pages, such as Advanced Event Systems results pages, when the Club chooses to. Those services aren't ours: we don't guarantee they will be available or accurate, and imports can stop working if they change.

16. Acceptable use

The acceptable-use rules in our general Terms of Service apply to the Club and its staff. We may suspend a team or a Club that breaks them, and will tell the director why.

17. Support and availability

We support Clubs directly by email at support@scoreframe.app and aim to fix serious problems quickly. ScoreFrame depends on phones, networks, app stores, YouTube and other services we don't control, so we can't promise it will always be available or error-free.

To the extent the law allows, we disclaim implied warranties, and our total liability to the Club for any claim is limited to the fees the Club paid us in the 12 months before the claim. We aren't liable for indirect or consequential losses, such as lost games, recordings or streams.

19. Indemnity

20. Ending this agreement

The Club can stop using club licenses at any time; licenses already bought run to their end date. We can end this agreement with 30 days' notice, or immediately for a serious breach, and will refund unused time if we end it without cause. Section 10 applies to the Club's data afterwards.
