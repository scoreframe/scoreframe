  • Licenses don't renew by themselves. We remind you before they end.
  • Card and bank payments activate your licenses right away. Paying by invoice covers up to 3 teams until the invoice is paid.
  • Your rosters, schedules, stats and team pages are yours. Videos live on your own YouTube channels.
  • We hold only players' names, numbers, positions and stats; outside a team's own staff, players show as first name and last initial. We don't host photos or video.
  • Videos live on YouTube. Whoever owns the YouTube channel is responsible for its videos, their privacy settings and what's in them.
  • No automatic refunds. We look at refund requests case by case and may refund part of a license.
  • When licenses end, pages stay up read-only for a year, then your data is kept six more months for download before it's deleted. After a trial with no purchase, it's kept 60 days. We email you before each step.

1. These terms

These Club Terms are an agreement between Glavin Labs LLC, which makes ScoreFrame ("we", "us"), and the organization named on the club's account (the "Club"). They cover club licenses bought on scoreframe.app. ScoreFrame's general Terms of Service and Privacy Policy also apply to everyone who uses ScoreFrame; where they conflict with these Club Terms on a club matter, these Club Terms control.

2. Who agrees

The person who creates the club, or accepts these terms in the club setup, confirms that they are authorized to agree for the Club. That person is the club's director. The Club is responsible for the directors, coaches and managers it invites, and for keeping its director accounts secure. Club licenses are for organizations, not for personal or household use. We record which version of these terms each director accepted, when, and from which account.

3. Licenses

  • A license covers one team for the period bought. A Club can't have more active teams than it has licenses.
  • Everyone on a covered team's staff (director, coaches and managers) gets ScoreFrame Pro features while using that team. It doesn't change their plan for other teams.
  • The director can move licenses between the Club's teams. Licenses can't be resold or used for teams outside the Club.
  • The minimum purchase is 3 teams.

4. Free trial

A new Club can try ScoreFrame free for 14 days with up to 3 teams. Each organization gets one trial. If a trial looks like a repeat of an earlier one, we may ask for a card before it starts; the card isn't charged during the trial. When the trial ends without a purchase, see section 10.

5. Prices and payment

  • Prices are in US dollars, per team per month, and are shown before you buy. Buying 6 months or more costs less per month.
  • The price per team you first buy at is locked for 12 months from that first purchase, including when you add teams or renew in that time.
  • Sales tax is added where it applies. Payments are processed by Stripe; we don't store card or bank details.
  • Card payments activate licenses right away. US bank payments cover up to 3 teams while the payment clears (usually a few business days), then all licensed teams.

6. Invoices

A Club can ask to pay by invoice, for example with a purchase order. Invoices are due within 30 days. Until an invoice is paid, up to 3 of the Club's teams are covered; the rest are covered once it's paid. If an invoice is still unpaid 14 days after its due date, coverage from it stops until it's paid. We may agree in writing to cover all teams before payment, for example for a large club.

7. Ending and renewing

Licenses end on the date shown and don't renew by themselves. We email the director before coverage ends. The Club can renew, extend or add teams at any time on its club page.

8. Refunds

There are no automatic refunds. If something isn't working, contact us first: we'll work to fix it quickly. We consider refund requests individually and may refund part of a license, prorated by unused time and licenses. A full refund ends the licenses it paid for.

9. Your data

The Club owns the content it and its staff add to ScoreFrame: team names, rosters, schedules, scores, stats, notes and logos ("Club Content"). The Club gives us permission to store, process and display Club Content to provide ScoreFrame, following the Club's privacy settings. We don't sell Club Content. The director can download a copy of the Club's data at any time from the club page, and can also ask us for one.

10. Keeping and deleting data

  • A trial that ends without a purchase: coaches can no longer add games, scores or rosters to the Club's teams. We keep the Club's data for 60 days so the Club can buy and carry on, and the director can download it during that time. After that it's deleted.
  • A paid Club whose licenses end: the Club's pages stay up, read-only, for 12 months (clubs often pause between seasons); coaches can no longer add games, scores or rosters. After 12 months the pages go offline and we keep the data 6 more months so the director can download it. After that it's deleted.
  • Notice: we email the Club's directors and billing contact when licenses end and several more times before pages go offline and before data is deleted, more often as each date nears. Data is never deleted sooner than 14 days after our first such email.
  • Renewing or buying licenses at any time before deletion restores the Club's teams as they were.
  • When the Club's data is deleted, teams the Club created are deleted with it. A team that joined the Club from a coach's own account goes back to that coach. Coaches' and families' own ScoreFrame accounts are not affected. Videos on YouTube are controlled by their channel owners, not by us.

11. Privacy settings

The director chooses who can see the Club's team pages, game pages and tournament pages: only followers a coach approves, anyone signed in to ScoreFrame, or anyone with the link. New clubs start at "anyone signed in to ScoreFrame". Each team follows the club setting unless it's made more private; coaches can make their own team more private but not more open. Note that anyone can create a free ScoreFrame account, so "signed in" limits casual sharing and keeps pages out of search engines, but it is not the same as approving each person.

12. Players and children

ScoreFrame stores only what staff enter about players: name, jersey number, position and game stats. We don't store player photos or video. Anywhere a player can be seen by people outside the team's own staff and approved followers, ScoreFrame shows only their first name and last initial; the full name is kept separately and shown only to staff and approved followers. Club teams often include children, so the Club confirms, when it accepts these terms, that it has the permission it needs from parents or guardians to list its players and to record and stream its games, and it will remove a player's information when a parent asks. A parent or guardian can also ask us at support@scoreframe.app; we act within 30 days and tell the Club. ScoreFrame accounts are for people 13 and older; players' information is entered by Club staff, not by children.

13. YouTube and video

Video is not hosted by ScoreFrame. Games are streamed and stored on YouTube channels that the Club or its staff own. The owner of each channel is solely responsible for the videos on it: their privacy settings (public, unlisted or private), who appears in them, permission to record and publish the people in them (including children), and everything else in them. ScoreFrame only sends the video to the channel the owner chose and links to it.

Connecting a channel on the club page lets ScoreFrame create live streams on that channel for the Club's teams; the connection is kept on our servers and is not shown to coaches. The Club can disconnect at any time on the club page or in its Google account settings. YouTube's Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy apply to anything on YouTube. The Club is responsible for its channel's settings and for having the right to record and stream its games, including venue rules and music.

A coach can give one game to another phone by QR code (for example a parent filming from the stands). That phone streams to the team's channel and keeps score for that game only, can't change the team, and keeps nothing about it afterwards. The Club is responsible for whom its coaches give games to.

14. AI tools

Some features read schedules, rosters and tournament draws from text, photos or files using AI services provided by third parties. Files are sent to those services only to produce the import and aren't used by us for anything else. Results can be wrong: review imports before relying on them. To keep costs fair, AI features have usage limits.

15. Results from other services

ScoreFrame can import schedules and results from public tournament pages, such as Advanced Event Systems results pages, when the Club chooses to. Those services aren't ours: we don't guarantee they will be available or accurate, and imports can stop working if they change.
