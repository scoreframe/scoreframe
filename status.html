iOS parity backlog Complete — 10 of 10

Done. Highlight Editor, the upload cluster, both stream fixes, bowling player favourites, end-game ordering and the post-game upload prompt have all shipped. The controller reel prompt turned out not to be missing at all — the full camera↔controller round trip already existed on iOS and matches Android; the backlog entry was simply wrong.

iOS edit → manage-overlay migration Audited — no gaps left

Functionality is moving out of the team Edit screen into the MANAGE THIS TEAM overlay, and the port stalled part-way. Sharing was the casualty — there was no way to publish a team at all, and the banner pointed at a control that didn't exist. Fixed in 1.2.170, along with an editor that replaced itself with a spinner the moment you cleared the team name. Not user-facing — the App Store build still has the old working Edit path. All three overlays have now been audited line-by-line against Android and match, and every hub-management action (Visibility, Managers, Coaches, Subscribers, Sync Roster, Transfer Ownership, Remove Hub) is present on iOS. Sharing was the only casualty.

Server-side share limits May be unnecessary

The caps are still a UX gate only. But once lazy minting is on production, most of the need disappears — so this is worth revisiting after adoption, not before.
