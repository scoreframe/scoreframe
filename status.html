iOS 607 crashes on launch — fixed in code, not yet re-shipped 1 decision

I shipped 607 to TestFlight having only ever launched the app in the simulator. On a real phone it dies instantly, every time.

Cause: HomeScreen.collapsibleHomeSection was one ~120-line VStack. Its generated type nested deep enough that the Swift runtime blew the stack just decoding it — SIGSEGV before the first frame. Pre-existing, not from tonight's edits; the simulator never reproduces it, which is how it got out.

Fixed and verified on your iPhone SE: each branch is type-erased before composition, the app launches, runs and syncs. Needs a bump to 608 and a re-upload — say go and it's done.

iOS coach marks — actually anchored nowverified by screenshot

Mark 1 was a centred bubble with no cutout because the Account tab lives in the bottom nav, outside the scroll view, so its position never reached the overlay. Mark 2 framed RECENT GAMES instead of the card above it — the overlay was drawing in its own space and every cutout sat one top-bar lower than it should. Both fixed; positions are now resolved when each step is drawn, the way Android has always done it. All five in-game marks fire after rotating too.

iOS orientation lock leaknot device-verified

A dialog opening during a landscape recording could pin the whole app to portrait — it was reading the physical device orientation, which reports "unknown" when the phone is flat or on a simulator, and defaulting to portrait. Now reads what the app is actually displaying, and End Game always releases the lock. Worth 30 seconds on your phone — record in landscape, end the game, rotate.

Android — permissions moved where you askedyour call, done

All of them now fire once, right after the intro pages: camera, mic, notifications, storage. Tapping outside no longer ends the flow — you get a non-dismissable dialog naming exactly what's still needed. This also fixes the missing notification prompt: the old chain that asked for it was only reachable by a path a fresh install no longer took.

Android — Google auto sign-in was silently blockedregression, fixed

Auto sign-in waits for the first-run tour to complete. That flag used to be set by the old tour dialog, which the new coach marks replaced — so on a fresh install it was never set and auto sign-in was blocked permanently, with nothing on screen to show for it.

You could share a team but never un-share ityou found this

This morning's sport-lock copy told people to "stop sharing first" — an action that did not exist anywhere in the app. The function was written and correct but had zero callers; its UI was deleted in v14 because every team auto-minted a share code back then. Lazy minting ended that on 5 Aug and nobody revisited it. Stop sharing is back, in the Sharing section where it belongs. Nothing is deleted — the page comes down, the link stops working, and the sport unlocks.

"Danger Zone" renamed on both platformsyour call, done

Two different sections shared that heading, so it identified neither. Now Archive or Delete and Fix or Stop Sharing — named for what they do. The scary part belongs in the confirmation, which is where it is.
