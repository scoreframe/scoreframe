Deleted games came back — and refresh could never remove onein v1180

You deleted 3 games on iOS and they stayed on Android through a restart, a Sync Now and a pull to refresh. The refresh path was add-only: it downloaded games and re-uploaded local ones, but nothing in it ever deleted. Worse, that re-upload pushed games you'd deleted elsewhere back to the cloud — almost certainly the long-running "deleted games come back" complaint. Refresh now applies deletions first and never re-uploads something you deleted.

Tennis and pickleball never attached to a teamin v1180

Whatever team you picked, those two sports always saved with none — so "this game isn't attached to a team" on upload, and the game stayed on your device. Ten sports read the team you selected; tennis and pickleball read fields nothing in the app ever sets. The team is now attached in ONE place for every sport, so sport #13 can't repeat it. Existing tennis/pickleball games are still unattached — recovering them needs a separate pass.

Asked to share a team you'd just sharedin v1180 + iOS

The lacrosse one, and you were right that it was a race. Sharing wrote the code to the cloud but not to the local copy, and every "is this shared?" check reads local — so for a few seconds the app sincerely believed an already-shared team wasn't, asked again, and called the game device-only. Both platforms now write it locally before returning.

You couldn't stop sharing a teamboth platforms

You spotted this: the sport-lock message told people to un-share, and there was no way to. The function existed with zero callers — its UI was deleted back when every team auto-minted a code, and lazy minting ended that on 5 Aug without anyone revisiting it. It's back under Sharing → Fix or Stop Sharing.
