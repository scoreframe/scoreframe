3. Camps, leaders and roles

  • Visitors have limited access. They can't see anyone's location, post in camp chat, or edit most things.

Anyone with a camp's join code can join while the code is valid, so share it only with family. Leaders can remove members at any time. If the last leader leaves, we may make another member a leader so the camp isn't left without one.

We aren't part of your family's decisions. We don't settle disagreements between members about a camp, its content or who belongs in it.

  • Use it only with people who know and agree to it.

  • KinCamp isn't an emergency service or a safety device. Don't rely on it to know where someone is in an emergency. Call 911.
